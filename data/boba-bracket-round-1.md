# Boba Bracket: Round 1 Plan

Updated November 18, 2026 · Organizers only, please don't share with tasters

Twelve shops, 21 tasters, 24 drinks and about two hours: two group rounds run at two tables at once, then one final for everyone. Every cup is blind; tasters only ever see letters.

## Panels and schedule

Tasters are split at random into Red (5), Blue (5), Green (5) and Yellow (6). Each panel judges two neighbouring groups, so every group gets two panels and no single panel decides a group.

| Round | Table 1 | Table 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (~15 min) | Group 1: Red + Blue (10 votes) | Group 3: Green + Yellow (11 votes) |
| 2 (~15 min) | Group 2: Blue + Green (10 votes) | Group 4: Yellow + Red (11 votes) |
| Break (~10 min) | App scores the 4 groups | |
| Final (~15 min) | All 21 rank the 4 group winners | |
| Reveal | Unmask round by round, champion last | |

Each taster tastes 10 samples of about 25 mL: 6 in the groups, 4 in the final.

## Scoring

1. A beats B if more tasters ranked A above B; the most head-to-head wins takes the group or the final.
2. Two shops level on wins: their own head-to-head decides.
3. Still level, or three shops loop: points (3/2/1 in a group, 4/3/2/1 in the final).
4. Still level: most 1st-place votes.

## Roles and blinding

Everyone tastes, including helpers, so the secret is split between two people.

- **Bagger (1):** takes bags from drivers, gives each shop a secret number 1–12, and pours cups marked by number. Never sees letters.
- **Labellers (2, one per table):** cover numbers with that round's random letters and set out cups. Never see bags or shop names.
- **Drivers (5):** hand bags straight to the bagger.
- Letters change every round and are never reused. Placemat orders rotate so each cup sits in each position equally often.
- Don't share the shop list or the group themes with tasters before the reveal.

## Numbers and supplies
